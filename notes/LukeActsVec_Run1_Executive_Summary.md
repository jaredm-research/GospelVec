# LukeActsVec Run 1 — Executive Summary

LukeActsVec Run 1 successfully adapted the GospelVec workflow from Matthew/Mark/Luke/John to Matthew/Mark/Luke/Acts using Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B on a Runpod RTX 4090. The first-pass result did **not** recover positive Luke–Acts affinity. Instead, Matthew, Mark, and Luke formed a positively aligned synoptic cluster, while Acts separated strongly from all three at the best-performing layer. The peak layer was **21**, with a run-level classification accuracy of **0.650655** under the repository’s current evaluation procedure. A supplementary cross-run John–Acts comparison gave a weakly positive cosine of **+0.104102**, suggesting mild alignment in structural role or direction, but not strong direct affinity. The current result is best understood as a meaningful first-pass activation-space geometry result with clear signal, but with methodological caveats that justify a narrower and more rigorous follow-up experiment, especially **Luke vs Acts only**.
